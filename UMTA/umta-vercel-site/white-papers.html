White Papers

Legal writing by UMTA members

Mooting sharpens oral advocacy, but great advocates write too. This is where UMTA publishes legal papers researched and written by our members: case comments, Charter analyses, and deep dives into the issues we argue in competition.

Our first papers are on the way

The inaugural collection of UMTA white papers is currently in research and review. Check back soon, or follow us on Instagram to be notified when the first papers are published.

Write with us

Any UMTA member can propose a paper. Pick a case or legal question you care about, and our executive team will help you refine and publish it.

Build your portfolio

A published paper is a serious asset for law school applications. Show admissions committees you can research, reason, and write.

Get started

Send your topic proposal to umta@uwindsor.ca with the subject line "White Paper Proposal".

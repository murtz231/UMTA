Tournament Calendar

Undergraduate moots and mock trials across Canada

Every undergraduate moot and mock trial tournament we know of, in one place. Ontario first, then the biggest competitions elsewhere in Canada. Built for our own members, and open to any undergraduate student in the country looking for their next competition.

Undergraduate only. Law school competitions are not listed here. Everything below is open to undergraduate students.

Most clubs announce dates only a few weeks ahead, so recurring tournaments without a confirmed date are marked TBA with a contact you can reach out to directly. Dates and links were last checked on 16 November 2026.

Hosting something we have missed, or spotted an error? Email umta@uwindsor.ca and we will add or correct it.
